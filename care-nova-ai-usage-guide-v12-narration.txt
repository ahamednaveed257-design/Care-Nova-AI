Welcome to Care Nova AI, built by Naveed Ahamed.

This walkthrough shows how to use the agent safely, step by step.

Follow the visual pointer, listen to the short explanation, then use the same flow in the app.

Step 1. Start here: Care Nova is a private workspace for asking, reviewing, saving, and sharing safer health information.

Step 2. Set the privacy base first: local records are the trusted memory that all agents reuse.

Step 3. Build the patient profile before asking questions so every answer has useful context.

Step 4. Use one clear question with timing, severity, readings, medicines, and warning signs.

Step 5. Every request moves through memory, intent routing, one specialist agent, safety, reply, and saved memory.

Step 6. Use General first when you are unsure where to begin or need a plain next step.

Step 7. Use Specialist when the concern belongs to a core disease area or body system.

Step 8. Use Atlas when you want to learn from ready guides before taking the next step.

Step 9. Use Vitals to turn readings into patterns, not isolated numbers.

Step 10. Use Medicine to prepare safe pharmacist or clinician questions without changing doses yourself.

Step 11. Use Labs to convert report values into simple explanations and follow-up questions.

Step 12. Use Wellness to build age-aware daily habits that can be saved as feedback.

Step 13. Use Visits to prepare booking details, doctor questions, and follow-up notes.

Step 14. Use Safety whenever urgent warning signs may be present.

Step 15. Use Insurance to organize claim evidence, benefits, documents, and appeal questions.

Step 16. Use Records after every meaningful check so the next run has stronger memory.

Step 17. Use Summary before visits or sharing with a care team.

Step 18. Switch Guided and Expert based on how much detail the user needs.

Step 19. Use online enrichment when available, but keep the local record as the trusted source.

Step 20. Save useful answers and feedback so routing and summaries become more precise.

Step 21. Daily best flow: profile, choose tab, ask once, review safety, save the output.

Step 22. Remember the boundary: Care Nova supports education, organization, and safe next steps.

That is the complete Care Nova workflow.

Use one focused tab at a time, review safety, and save useful results to local records.
